Section Element

It represents a generic standalone section of a document. A section should always have a heading, a very few exceptions. It should only be used if there isn't a more specific element to represent it. As an example, a navigation menu should be wrapped in a <nav> element, but a list of search results or a map display and its controls don't have specific elements and could be put inside a <section>.
Syntax- 

            <section>Content</section>
Example-
<section>
<h2>Lorem, ipsum.</h2>
<p> Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipisicing elit. Libero voluptatibus maxime ab iste similique, et enim. Accusamus, earum ratione blanditiis corrupti enim eius, dicta consequuntur inventore quo ad tempore pariatur sequi. Sed architecto, ullam iure labore cupiditate mollitia animi fuga sit quis itaque. </p>
</section>

Output-

Lorem, ipsum.

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipisicing elit. Libero voluptatibus maxime ab iste similique, et enim. Accusamus, earum ratione blanditiis corrupti enim eius, dicta consequuntur inventore quo ad tempore pariatur sequi. Sed architecto, ullam iure labore cupiditate mollitia animi fuga sit quis itaque.
