Symbols

HTML symbols are special characters used to display characters not on the keyboard or that might conflict HTML code. It uses name entities and number entities.

Here are some commonly used symbols in the below code-

© - This is the copyright sign, indicates that the creator owns the rights to a work, protecting it from unauthorized use or reproduction.
Example-
© 2026 Your Company. All rights reserved.


® - This is the registered trademark sign, shows that a brand, logo, or name is officially registered with the government and legally protected from unauthorized use.
Example-
® Your Brand Name


™ - This is the trademark symbol, indicates that a word, logo, symbol is being claimed as a trademark, even if it is not officially registered.
Example-
™ Your Product Name


@ - This is the "at" sign. It is commonly used in email addresses to separate the username from the domain and also in social media to mention or tag someone.
Example-
@username
