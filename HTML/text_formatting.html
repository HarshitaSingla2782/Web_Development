Text Formatting Tags

Text formatting refers to the use of specific HTML tags to modify the appearance and structure of text on a web page. It allows you to style text in different ways, such as making it bold, italic, underline, highlighted, or struck- through.

Bold- The <b> tag visually makes the text bold but does not imply any special significance.
Example-
<b>Bold</b>

Output-
Bold


Strong- The <strong> tag is semantically meaningful and indicates that the text is of high importance.
Example-
<strong>Strong</strong>

Output-
Strong


Italics- Use the <i> tag to display text in italics without implying in emphasis.
Example-
<i>Italics</i>

Output-
Italics


Emphasis- The <em> tag is used for emphasis text and is usually rendered in the italics to highlight importance.
Example-
<em>Emphasis</em>

Output-
Emphasis


Underline- The <u> tag represents some text that is unarticulated and styled differently from normal text, such as misspelled words or proper name. The content inside is typically displayed with an underline.
Example-
<u>Underline</u>

Output-
Underline


Insert- The <ins> tag marks text as newly added or inserted, often displayed with an underline.
Example-
<ins>Insert</ins>

Output-
Insert


Mark- The <mark> tag highlights the text with a background color, similar to using a highlighter on paper.
Example-
<mark>Mark & Highlighted</mark>

Output-
Mark & Highlighted


Delete- The <del> tag is used to show that text has been deleted or no longer relevant.
Example-
<del>Deleted</del>

Output-
Deleted


Superscript- Uses the <sup> tag to show superscripted text, commonly used in exponents or footnotes.
Example-
(a + b)<sup>2</sup>

Output-
(a + b)2


Subscript- Uses the <sub> tag for subscripted text, often used in chemical formulas or footnotes.
Example-
H<sub>2</sub>O

Output-
H2O


Big- Uses the <big> tag to render the text in bigger font than the surrounding text.
Example-
<big>Big</big>

Output-
Big


Small- The <small> tag is used to display text in a smaller font size than the surrounding text.
Example-
<small>Small</small>

Output-
Small
