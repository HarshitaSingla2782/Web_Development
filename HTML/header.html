Header Element

The <header> element represents introductory content, typically a group of introductory or navigational aids. It may contain some heading elements but also a logo, a search form, an author name, and other elements..
Syntax- 

            <header>
                Headings
                Logo
            </header>
Example-
<header>
<h1>Lorem ipsum dolor sit.</h1>
</header>
<article>Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur, adipisicing elit. Recusandae provident officiis corrupti eveniet deleniti?</article>

Output-

Lorem ipsum dolor sit.

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur, adipisicing elit. Recusandae provident officiis corrupti eveniet deleniti?
