Chocolate Fudge Cake Recipe

Description: A rich, moist chocolate fudge cake perfect for birthdays and special occasions!

Ingredients:

  • 200g plain flour
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g unsalted butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100g dark chocolate (melted)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease and line a cake tin.
  2. Mix butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, beating well.
  4. Fold in flour, baking powder, cocoa powder, and vanilla.
  5. Stir in melted chocolate.
  6. Pour mixture into the tin and bake for 30-35 minutes.
  7. Allow to cool, then frost with your favorite chocolate icing. Enjoy!
