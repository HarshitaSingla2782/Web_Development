Food🍰🍦🍽

Food for a birthday party is all about celebration, fun, and flavor! It usually includes a mix of sweet and savory items that guests of all ages can enjoy. Popular choices are birthday cake, cupcakes, chips, pizza, finger foods, mocktails, and themed snacks. The menu is often colorful, easy to serve, and tailored to the party theme or guest preferences. Good food adds joy to the occasion and helps create lasting memories.

Kadi Chawal

A traditional North Indian comfort dish made with tangy yogurt-based curry and steamed rice. It’s wholesome, flavorful, and brings a homey warmth to any party platter.

Pani Puri

A burst of tangy, spicy, and sweet flavors in one bite, pani puri is the ultimate street food that brings excitement and fun to any birthday party.

Sevpuri

This crisp and colorful chaat, topped with sev, chutneys, and onions, adds a crunchy and zesty twist to your food table. A snack everyone loves!

Lemonade

Refreshing and zesty, lemonade is the perfect cooler for a sunny celebration. It balances sweetness with a citrusy punch and keeps everyone energized.

Cake

No birthday is complete without a cake! Whether chocolate, vanilla, or fruit-filled, cake brings sweetness and smiles to the celebration.

Amritsari Chole Kulcha

Spicy chickpeas paired with fluffy kulchas, this Punjabi delicacy brings a burst of bold flavors and hearty satisfaction to any birthday meal.

Spicy Honey Chilli Potato

Crispy potato fingers tossed in a sweet and spicy sauce with bell peppers—this Indo-Chinese dish adds a fiery kick to your party spread.

Masala Chaas

This spiced buttermilk drink cools you down and complements spicy dishes. Flavored with roasted cumin and fresh coriander, it's a refreshing twist on a traditional drink.

Thin Crust Veg. Pizza

Topped with fresh veggies and melting cheese on a crunchy crust, this pizza is a go-to crowd-pleaser, loved by kids and adults alike
