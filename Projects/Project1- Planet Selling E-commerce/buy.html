Before purchasing any planet, let's learn about what planets are. A planet is a celestial body that is in orbit around the sun. It has sufficient mass for its self- gravity to overcome the rigid body forces so that it assumes a hydrostatic equilibrium (nearly round) shape, and has cleared the neighborhood around its orbit.
A dwarf planet is a celestial body that is in orbit around the Sun. It has sufficient mass for its self- gravity to overcome the rigid body forces so that it assumes a hydrostatic equilibrium (nearly round) shape and has not cleared the neighborhood around its orbit, and is not a satellite.
