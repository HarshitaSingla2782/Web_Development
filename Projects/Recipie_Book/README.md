The project "RECIPIE BOOK" is all about different types of recipies where you can view the the recipie list, its category, and its full description. Here you can also add a new recipie of your choice with its name, image, ingredients, steps, time required to prepare the recipie, difficulty level, and nutrition value through forms. The project is made with pure HTML and the CSS is used only to give the borders and making the alignment of the text wherever needed. The project also includes special entities and semantic elements of HTML.
