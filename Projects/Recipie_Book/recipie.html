Poha

Ingredients Amount
Poha(Flattened Rice) 2 cup
Onion(finely chopped) 1
Potato(optional, diced small) 1
Green Chillies(chopped) 2
Curry Leaves 8-10
Mustard Seeds 1/2 tsp
Turmeric Powder(Haldi) 1/2 tsp
Salt To taste
Peanuts 1tbsp
Oil 2tbsp
Lemon Juice 1tbsp
Fresh Coriander Leaves 1/4 cup

Instructions:
    Prepare the Poha
  1. Use thick poha, as it absorbs water better and stays firm without turning mushy. Thin poha will become soggy and may not work well for this recipie.
  2. Wash it gently under running watter for 1-2 minutes. Use your fingers to lightly move the poha around while rinsing. The goal is to remove any dust and slightly soften the poha. Do not over- wash or soak it in water.
  3. After rinsing, let the poha sit un the sieve for 5-10 minutes. The water will drain out, and the poha will become soft and fluffy but not sticky. Test by pressing a flake between your fingers, it should break easily without feeling dry.
  4. Sprinkle a pinch of salt on the rinsed poha and gently mixed it with your hands. This ensures the poha is evenly seasoned and absorbs flavor during cooking. Now, the poha is prepared and ready to be added to the tempering and spices.

    5. Make the Tempering
  5. Place a wide pan or kadhai on medium heat and add 2 tablespoons of oil. Allow the oil to heat up. You can test it by dropping in one mustard seed, if it splutters, the oil is ready, Add mustard seeds to the hot oil. Let them splutter for a few seconds. This releases their aroma and adds a nutty flavor.
  6. Add 2 finely chopped green chillies to the pan. Stir them for 30 seconds. This step helps release their spice and aroma into the oil. If you prefer less spice, reduce the quantity of chillies or remove the seeds.
  7. Add 8-10 curry leaves at this stage. They add a wonderful aroma and traditional touch to the dish. Let them crackle in tbe oil for a fewseconds.

    8. Cook Onions and potatoes
  8. After the tempering is ready, add 1 medium sized onion, finely chopped, to the pan. Stir well so the onioin get coated with the flavored oil.
  9. Cook on medium heat, stirring occassionally, until the onions turn soft and traslucent. This usually takes 2-3 minutes. Sprinkle a small pinch of salt over the onions. This helps them cook faster and enhances their flavor.
  10. Add 1 medium sized potato, finely chopped into small cubes, to the pan. Mix well with the onions and tempering, so the potatoes are evenly coated with the oil and spices.
  11. Cover the pan with a lid and let the potatoes cook on low- medium heat. Stir occassionally to prevent sticking or burning. Cook until the potatoes are soft and tender. This takes about 5-7 minutes, depending on the size of cubes.
  12. To check if the potatoes are cooked, pierce a cube with a fork or knife, it should slide in easily. Once the onions are golden and the potatoes are fully cooked, give everything a final stir.The mixture should look evenly mixed and fragrant.

    13. Add Poha
  13. Once the onions and potatoes are cooked, reduce the heat to low. Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder evenly over the mixture. Stir gently so the turmeric blends well with the oil, onions, and potatoes. Cook for 30 seconds to release the aroma of turmeric.
  14. Add salt to taste at this stage. Remember, you already sprinkled a liitle salt on the poha earlier, so adjust accordingly. Now, gently add the softened poha to the pan.
  15. Stir slowly and carefully, ensuring the poha mixes evenly with the turmeric and onion- potato mixture. Avoid stirring too hard, as poha flakes can break easily.
  16. Let the poha cook on low heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring occassionally. This helps it absorb the flavors and ensures everything is heated through.

    17. Finish & Serve
  17. Once the poha is mixed well with the onion- potato base, add 1 tablespoon roasted peanuts. Stir gently to evenly distribute the peanuts throughout the poha. The peanuts add a delightful crunch and a nutty flavor to the dish.
  18. Switch off the flame to prevent the poha from overcooking or drying out. Squeeze the juice of 1 lemon(about 2 tablespoons) over the poha. Stir lightly to mix. Lemon juice adds a tangy freshness that balances the flavors beautifully.
  19. Sprinkle 1/4 cup chopped fresh coriander leaves over the poha. Coriander adds a burst of color and a fresh, herbaceous flavor.
  20. If the poha looks dry, sprinkle a tablespoon of water, mix gently, and let it sit covered for a minute before serving.

Your Kanda Poha is now ready—fluffy, flavorful, and filled with textures! Enjoy this delicious and easy Maharashtrian delicacy with family and friends.

Time: 20 mins
Difficulty: Medium   Medium

Tips:
  • Add a pinch of sugar for a slight sweet taste.
  • Serve with tea or chutney.
  • You can also add peas for extra flavor.

Kala Chana Salad

Ingredients Amount
Kala Chana(Black Chickpeas) 2 cup
Water 3 cup
Onion(finely chopped) 1
Cucumber(peeled & finely chopped) 1
Tomato(finely chopped) 1
Green Chillies(finely chopped) 2
Salt To taste
Cumin Powder(Jeera) 1/2 tsp
Lemon(Juice extracted) 1
Black Pepper Powder(Kaali Mirch) 1/2 tsp
Chaat Masala Powder 1/2 tsp
Coriander Leaves(Dhania) 1/4 cup

Instructions:
    Preparing Kala Chana
  1. Rinse kala chana well, then soak in plenty of water(8-10 hours). Drain, rinse again and add to pressure cooker with 3 cups of fresh water and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
  2. Pressure cook on high heat for 6 whistles, lower heat and cook 20 minutes more and turn off the heat. Let the pressure release naturally; the chickpeas should mash softly between fingers yet hold shape. Drain and cool completely.

    3. Preparing vegetables
  3. While chana cools, finely chop onion, tomato, cucumber, and green chillies. Keep tomato seeds minimal so the salad stays dry and fresh.

    4. Making Zesty Lemon dressing
  4. In a small bowl whisk lemon juice withand remaining salt until spices dissolve.

    5. Finish & Serve
  5. In a large mixing bowl, combine cooled chana, chopped veggies, green chillies, lemon, roasted cumin powder, chaat masala, black pepper, and coriander leaves. Combine well and check the salt and adjust according to taste.
  6. Serve this kala chana salad immediately at room temperature, or chill for 30 minutes for flavors to meld.

You Kala chana salad is now ready- packed with protien, rich in iron and full of fibre with nutty flavor- firm texture! Enjoy this delecious and easy Indian Style street food with dal and fluffy phulkas.

Time: 15 mins
Difficulty: Easy   Easy

Tips:
  • To begin making the Indian Style Black Chickpea Salad, prep all the ingredients and keep them ready for easy cooking.
  • Add boiled corn or pomengrante seeds for sweetness.
  • sprinkle sev or roasted peanuts for crunch.
  • For a spicy twist, add a pinch of red chilli powder.
  • Want a tangier flavor? Add a bit of tamrind(Imli) chutney.

Green Moong Dal

Ingredients Amount
Green Moong Dal(whole moong) 1 cup
Onion(finely chooped) 1
Tomato(chopped) 1
Green Chilli(finely chopped, optional) 1
Ginger- Garlic Paste 1 tsp
Clove 2
Turmeric Powder(Haldi) 1/2 tsp
Cumin Seeds(Jeera) 1 tsp
Red Chilli Powder 1/2 tsp
Coriander Powder(Dhania Powder) 1 tsp
Salt To taste
Ghee 1 tbsp
Water 3 cups
Coriander Leaves(Dhania) 1/4 cup

Instructions:
    Soak & Cook Dal
  1. Wash green moong dal well in running water.
  2. Soak for 15 minutes in a bowl.
  3. Rinse it with clear water once.
  4. Pressure cook with 3 cups of water. Add little salt, turmeric powder and give 4-5 whistles until soft.

    5. Make Masala
  5. Heat ghee in a pan on low heat.
  6. Add clove, and cumin seeds. Wait until slightly cook.
  7. Add onion and saute until golden.
  8. Add ginger- garlic paste, green chilli. Mix well all the ingredients until ginger- garlic paste got meged with all ingredients.
  9. Add tomato and mix well once with all the ingredients. Cook until soft.
  10. Add red chilli powder, corianger powder, and little turmeric(if needed).

    11. Combine
  11. Add cooked moong dal with water.
  12. Mix well and simmer 5-10 minutes.
    13. Finish & Serve
  13. Adjust salt and consistency. Garnish with coriander leaves.

Your green moong dal is now ready. Serve it with roti or rice.

Time: 40 mins
Difficulty: Medium  

Tips:
  • Soak dal for 4-6 hours for faster cooking.
  • Adding heeng is optional. Helps improve taste and good for digestion.

Coffee flavour Ice- Cream

Ingredients Amount
Fresh Cream(chilled) 2 cups
Condensed Milk 1 cup
Instant Coffee Powder 2 tbsp
Warm Water 2 tbsp
Vanilla Essence(optional) 1 tsp
Cocoa Powder(optional, for mocha flavor) 1 tbsp
Chocolate Chips or Crushed Biscuits(optional) Randomly sprinkle

Instructions:
    Prepare Coffee Mix
  1. Dissolve instant coffee powder in 2 tbsp warm water. Let it cool.

    2. Whip Cream
  2. In a chilled bowl, whip the cream until soft peaks form.

    3. Mix Base
  3. Add condensed milk, coffee mixture, and vanilla essence to the whipped cream. Fold gently.

    4. Add Extras
  4. Add chocolate chips or crushed biscuits if desired.

    5. Freeze
  5. Pour into an airtight container. Freeeze for 6-8 hours or overnight.

    6. Serve
  6. Scoop an enjoy. You can top it with cocoa powder or coffee drizzle.

Your coffee flavoured ice- cream is now ready. Enjoy it as a desert or a snack.

Time: 10 mins
Difficulty: Easy   Easy

Tips:
  • Use strong coffee for deeper flavour.
  • Add caramel sauce for coffee- caramel twist.
  • If you like bitterness, reduce condensed milk slightly.
